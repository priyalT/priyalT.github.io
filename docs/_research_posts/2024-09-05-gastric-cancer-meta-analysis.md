## Abstract & Summary

Gastric cancer remains a leading cause of oncological mortality worldwide, largely due to late-stage diagnosis. This presentation reported a meta-analytic approach to integrate disparate microarray and RNA-seq human genomic datasets from NCBI GEO and TCGA.

By aggregating cross-study differential gene expression statistics through random-effects modeling, we identified consensus transcriptional alterations in gastric adenocarcinoma tissue compared to adjacent normal mucosa. These candidate signatures were subsequently evaluated using supervised classification models to assess their predictive diagnostic power.
